How to play, Commander!

Type your name to see it here:

1Your big mission

Write letters like a rocket or a meteor falling from space.

Always start at the TOP and fall DOWN. ⬇️

2The green star

1
✅ Start at the star. Fall down!
1
✋
🛑 Starting at the bottom gets stopped.

Put your pencil on the green star. The star tells you where to start. If you touch the bottom, the app says "Rockets launch from the TOP!" and waits for you to try again.

3Stay on the road

Follow the road with your pencil. Slow and smooth wins. 🐢

Blue-purple ink = on the road ✅
Keep going!
Orange ink = off the road! 🟠
Your line gets erased. Lift your pencil and try that part again.

It is not a mistake. It is just practice. Try again until it stays on the road.

4Four levels

Every letter, word and sentence has 4 levels. The road gets less help each time. You open the next one by getting 3 stars ⭐⭐⭐ on the last one.

1 TraceFollow the glowing road.
🔒 2 Star dotsOnly the green start stars show.
🔒 3 Moon helperYou choose where to write.
🔒 4 SoloJust the lines. You are a pro!

A 🔒 means: win the level before it first.

5Your helper buttons

▶ Watch👀 Peek↺ Redo
  • Watch shows the meteor writing it for you. In Words it writes the whole word.
  • Peek shows the start stars for a moment. Look, then write it yourself!
  • Redo wipes the paper so you can try again.

6The three lines

Letters live on three lines, like on your school paper.

SPACE top line. Tall letters, capitals and numbers touch it.

ORBIT dotted middle line. Small letters like a, c, e reach it.

SURFACE bottom line. Every letter sits on it.

BLACK HOLE the grey space under it. Tails of g j p q y fall in!

7Words and spaces

🔤 Keep letters in a word close, like friends holding hands.

🌙 Between words, leave room for one moon (about the size of an o).

Sentences start with a Capital and end with a . (period). For stories, start a new line for each sentence.

⭐Super Words

Look for the ⭐ super words list in Words. These are words from your reading class, like the, said and because.

You get a new set every mission, so it never gets boring!

8Stars

When you finish, you earn up to 3 stars:

⭐⭐⭐ = Mastered! Every start at the top, on the road, good spacing.

⭐⭐ or ⭐ = good try! Do it again to get 3.

9Mission Control and fuel

Mission Control is a timed mission, about 15 minutes.

  • Tap ▶ Start Mission Control.
  • The app picks missions for you. Do your best writing!
  • Watch the fuel gauge fill up. ⛽ The white line is your target.
  • When the time is up, you get your fuel points if you reached the target.
The fuel gauge fills as you earn fuel points.

The clock stops if you stop writing for a while, so it is okay to take a short break. 😌

Your first Mission Control is a practice one. There is no target. Everything you earn goes in your bank!

10How to earn fuel points

You earn fuel only for a clean run = 3 stars ⭐⭐⭐ with no redo needed.

Harder levels give more fuel! Trace gives the least. Solo gives the most. 🚀

Two rules to keep it fair:

  • The same one only counts 2 times in a mission. Pick a new one!
  • Letters can only fill half the gauge. You need words and sentences too.

11The bank 🏦

Reached the target? 🎯
All your fuel goes into your bank!
Did not reach it?
No bank points this time. No big deal. Try again tomorrow!

Your bank keeps growing every time you reach the target. Your target will go up a little as you get better. That means you are improving! 💪

12Spend it: screen time 📺

Your bank points buy screen time.

2 points = 1 minute.

Tap 📺 Screen time and pick how long. The points come out of your bank and a big timer counts down. When it hits zero, it is time to come back to mission control!

Try it! How many points are in your bank?

If you stop early, the whole minutes you did not use come back to your bank.

⭐Remember these 4 things

  • Start at the TOP star.
  • Fall down and follow the road.
  • Leave one moon between words.
  • Go for a clean run (3 stars) to fill your fuel!
